## About

I'm a B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at VIT-AP University (CGPA 9.35/10) working across quantitative risk systems, graph-based drug discovery, and safety verification for autonomous systems. I move between research, engineering, and founding — writing the paper, shipping the pipeline, and building the company around it. My work leans on statistics over intuition: purged cross-validation, walk-forward backtests, and stress tests, because a result only matters next to what it beat.
